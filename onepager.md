# Distributor Orders

Consumer-goods manufacturers and their distributors need a dependable way to move orders from product selection to delivery. Scattered messages and separate records make availability, credit exceptions and loyalty rewards harder to follow.

Distributor Orders brings this work into one application. Distributors browse products, prices and available stock, edit a cart containing several products, and see the resulting total and status. Their current credit, recent loyalty points and tier are visible. They can review their own orders and cancel eligible orders before dispatch.

Sales Managers see all orders and a dedicated approval queue. They approve or reject credit exceptions, dispatch confirmed orders and record delivery. Order details show line items, fixed discounts and a dated history of status changes.

Stock is reserved only when the whole order can be fulfilled. Rejection or cancellation releases its reservation; dispatch permanently deducts stock. Delivered orders stop consuming credit. Confirmation awards points, cancellation reverses the original award, and recent points determine the discount tier for the next order.

New status changes also generate notifications for the manufacturer’s ERP, with retries for temporary failures. A local demonstration receiver is included; connecting a real ERP requires agreement with its owner.

The system replaces scattered ordering and manual follow-up with clearer commitments, consistent controls and shared visibility. The current demonstration uses seeded actors without login; production rollout and access controls remain separate work.
